Recommended Next Steps
Change Evaluation Metrics

Abandon accuracy, as it gives a false sense of performance on imbalanced datasets.

Evaluate using metrics that account for class imbalance, such as Balanced Accuracy, Macro/Weighted F1-score, Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC), or a Confusion Matrix.

Establish Baseline Comparisons

Compare performance against a dummy classifier (e.g., DummyClassifier(strategy="most_frequent") or strategy="stratified").

Address Overfitting & Imbalance

Regularization: Increase regularization penalties (e.g., L1/L2 weights, tree depth limits, or dropout rate) to narrow the gap between training and validation scores.

Resampling / Class Weights: Apply techniques like SMOTE, random undersampling, or class weight adjustments (class_weight='balanced') during training.
