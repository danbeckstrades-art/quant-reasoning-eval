The probability that a person who tests positive actually has the condition is $\frac{1}{6}$ (approximately 16.67%).Step-by-Step Calculation (Bayes' Theorem)1. Given ProbabilitiesPrevalence ($P(\text{Condition})$): $0.01$No Condition ($P(\text{No Condition})$): $1 - 0.01 = 0.99$Sensitivity ($P(\text{Positive} \mid \text{Condition})$): $0.99$Specificity ($P(\text{Negative} \mid \text{No Condition})$): $0.95$False Positive Rate ($P(\text{Positive} \mid \text{No Condition})$): $1 - 0.95 = 0.05$2. Total Probability of Testing PositiveA positive result can come from two sources: true positives (people with the condition) and false positives (people without the condition).$$P(\text{Positive}) = [P(\text{Positive} \mid \text{Condition}) \times P(\text{Condition})] + [P(\text{Positive} \mid \text{No Condition}) \times P(\text{No Condition})]$$$$P(\text{Positive}) = (0.99 \times 0.01) + (0.05 \times 0.99)$$$$P(\text{Positive}) = 0.0099 + 0.0495 = 0.0594$$3. Calculate Positive Predictive Value (PPV)Using Bayes' Theorem to find $P(\text{Condition} \mid \text{Positive})$:$$P(\text{Condition} \mid \text{Positive}) = \frac{P(\text{Positive} \mid \text{Condition}) \times P(\text{Condition})}{P(\text{Positive})}$$$$P(\text{Condition} \mid \text{Positive}) = \frac{0.0099}{0.0594} = \frac{99}{594} = \frac{1}{6} \approx 16.67\%$$Alternative Method: Population Intuition (Natural Frequencies)Imagine a group of 10,000 people:100 people have the condition ($1\%$).$99\%$ of them test positive $\rightarrow$ 99 true positives9,900 people do not have the condition ($99\%$).$5\%$ of them test positive $\rightarrow$ 495 false positives ($0.05 \times 9,900$)Total positive tests: $99 + 495 = 594$ people.Out of those 594 people with positive test results, only 99 actually have the condition:$$\frac{99}{594} = \frac{1}{6} \approx 16.67\%$$
