To detect an increase in conversion rate from 10% to 12% with 80% power and a 5% significance level ($\alpha = 0.05$), you need approximately 3,841 users per group (or ~7,682 total users).Key ParametersBaseline Conversion Rate ($p_1$): $0.10$Target Conversion Rate ($p_2$): $0.12$Absolute Effect Size ($\Delta = p_2 - p_1$): $0.02$Pooled Mean Conversion ($\bar{p} = \frac{p_1 + p_2}{2}$): $0.11$Significance Level ($\alpha$): $0.05 \implies Z_{\alpha/2} \approx 1.96$Power ($1 - \beta$): $0.80 \implies Z_{\beta} \approx 0.8416$Formula (Normal Approximation for Two Proportions)The standard formula accounting for pooled variance under $H_0$ and unpooled variance under $H_1$ is:$$n = \frac{\left( Z_{\alpha/2} \sqrt{2\bar{p}(1 - \bar{p})} + Z_{\beta} \sqrt{p_1(1 - p_1) + p_2(1 - p_2)} \right)^2}{(p_2 - p_1)^2}$$CalculationCalculate the variance components:Under $H_0$ (pooled):$$2\bar{p}(1 - \bar{p}) = 2 \times 0.11 \times 0.89 = 0.1958 \implies \sqrt{0.1958} \approx 0.44249$$Under $H_1$ (unpooled):$$p_1(1 - p_1) + p_2(1 - p_2) = (0.10 \times 0.90) + (0.12 \times 0.88) = 0.09 + 0.1056 = 0.1956 \implies \sqrt{0.1956} \approx 0.44227$$Multiply by the critical $Z$-values:$Z_{\alpha/2} \times \sqrt{2\bar{p}(1 - \bar{p})} = 1.96 \times 0.44249 \approx 0.86728$$Z_{\beta} \times \sqrt{p_1(1 - p_1) + p_2(1 - p_2)} = 0.8416 \times 0.44227 \approx 0.37221$Sum the numerator components and square:$$\text{Numerator} = (0.86728 + 0.37221)^2 = (1.23949)^2 \approx 1.53634$$Divide by the squared effect size ($\Delta^2 = 0.02^2 = 0.0004$):$$n = \frac{1.53634}{0.0004} \approx 3,840.85$$Rounding up to the nearest whole integer gives 3,841 users per group.(Note: Using the simplified unpooled formula $n = \frac{(Z_{\alpha/2} + Z_\beta)^2 \cdot [p_1(1-p_1) + p_2(1-p_2)]}{(p_2 - p_1)^2}$ yields $n \approx 3,839$ users per group).
