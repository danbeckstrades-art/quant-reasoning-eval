(b) What does AUC mean in terms of pairs of examples?

AUC is the probability that a randomly chosen positive example is assigned a higher score than a randomly chosen negative example.

So here:

AUC=0.8333\boxed{\text{AUC}=0.8333}AUC=0.8333​
