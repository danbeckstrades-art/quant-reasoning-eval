The Bonferroni correction divides the overall family-wise error rate by the number of tests:

αBonf=0.0520=0.0025.\alpha_{\text{Bonf}}=\frac{0.05}{20}=0.0025.αBonf​=200.05​=0.0025. αBonf=0.0025\boxed{\alpha_{\text{Bonf}}=0.0025}αBonf​=0.0025​
