Conclusion
The principal's conclusion is not justified. Since the students were chosen because they had unusually low initial scores, an average improvement was expected automatically due to regression to the mean. The observed gain of 8 points does not exceed the 9-point gain predicted from regression alone, so these data provide no evidence that the tutoring program improved scores. To assess effectiveness, the school would need a comparable control group of low-scoring students who were not tutored.
